Privacy Policy

Emberline Studio Pipeline · effective 19 September 2026

Who uses this app

One person: the owner of the YouTube channel The Psychological Audit, signed in to that channel's own Google account. The app has no other users, no sign-up and no public access.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, the app uses Google's YouTube APIs to read that channel's own video list, video details, YouTube Analytics and YouTube Reporting data, and, at the owner's direction, to update that channel's own video details, thumbnails, captions and comments. It accesses no other person's Google account or data.

What it collects about you

Nothing. This website sets no cookies, runs no scripts, and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. The app collects no personal information from visitors or viewers.

How the data is stored and used

The channel's own data is stored on the owner's own computer and is used only to operate and improve the channel. The owner may analyse it with software he runs himself, including AI-assisted tools. It is not sold, not shared for advertising, and not transferred to anyone else.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
