Emberline Studio Pipeline

Emberline Media is the production company behind the YouTube channel The Psychological Audit.

Emberline Studio Pipeline is a private, internal tool. The channel's owner uses it, signed in to the channel's own Google account, to read that channel's own YouTube statistics and to manage that channel's own videos. It is not a public service, it has no sign-up, and it is not offered to anyone else.
